Need help?

Frequently asked.

Are these flights bookable?+

No. Every route, price and schedule shown on this page is illustrative for the GED school project. No commercial transactions are processed.

How are the fares calculated?+

Fares are demo values set at build time, based on realistic economy-class pricing for each route. They do not reflect current market prices.

What is the GED Score?+

The GED Score combines price, duration, number of stops, on-time performance and CO₂ into a single 0–10 metric. Higher is better.

Why do some routes show "via PTY" or "via BOG"?+

Those are connections through Copa (Panama) and Avianca (Bogotá) hubs. They often offer lower fares at the cost of longer total travel time.

What does "All Origins" do?+

Selecting "All Origins" shows routes from every Brazilian airport we cover to the chosen destination — perfect for discovering new connection options.

Can I combine a flight with a hotel or package?+

Yes. Use the "Plan around this" button on any route or go directly to the trip builder to combine flights, hotels and experiences.
